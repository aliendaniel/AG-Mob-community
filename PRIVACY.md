# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AG Mob handles information when you use the app, contact support, or participate in this public community.

AG Mob is an independent companion app for connecting your iPhone or iPad to an OpenClaw Gateway that you own or are authorized to use. AG Mob is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or published by the OpenClaw project or its maintainers. The app is developed and published by Chengling Hua as an independent individual developer. It is designed to keep you in control of your Gateway connection, chat sessions, task history, approvals, attachments, and local settings.

## Summary

- AG Mob does not sell personal information.
- AG Mob does not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.
- The app connects to the Gateway you choose. Chat content, task content, attachments, and related metadata may be sent to that Gateway so the app can work.
- Sensitive connection credentials are stored locally using platform-provided secure storage where available.
- Camera, microphone, photos, files, location, and notifications are requested only for user-initiated features.
- Security and privacy issues should be reported privately to `aliendaniel@hotmail.com`.

## Information The App Handles

### Gateway Connection Information

To connect to your Gateway, the app may store and use:

- Gateway name
- Host, URL, and port
- Setup code details entered or scanned by you
- Device identity and pairing token
- Connection status, last sync time, and recovery state

This information is used to connect, reconnect, and recover from temporary offline states.

### Chat, Task, And Run Content

When you use AG Mob, the app may display, cache, and send the following to your selected Gateway:

- Chat messages and assistant responses
- Session titles, recent message previews, unread counts, and run status
- Task titles, task instructions, schedules, task status, and run history
- Approval requests and your approve/reject choices
- Process details, tool results, tables, long text, diagnostics, and attachments

This data is used to provide the core app experience: chats, tasks, live runs, readable results, approvals, search, and offline recovery.

### Attachments You Choose

If you choose to add attachments, the app may process the files or media you select, such as:

- Photos or videos from your photo library
- Photos taken with the camera
- Documents or other files selected through the file picker
- Voice recordings or dictated text
- Location information when you explicitly attach your current location

Attachments are used only for the chat or task workflow you choose.

### Local Settings And Preferences

The app may store local preferences such as:

- Language preference
- Notification permission state
- Device capability toggles
- Cached sessions, cached task lists, and pending recovery queue
- UI state such as selected filters or recent navigation state

These settings help the app restore your workspace and continue working after relaunch or reconnect.

### Support And Community Information

If you contact support by email or participate in GitHub Issues or Discussions, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as:

- Your email address
- Your GitHub username
- Device model, app version, OS version, and connection type
- Screenshots, recordings, logs, or diagnostic details you attach
- The content of your support request, bug report, or feature suggestion

Please do not include setup codes, device tokens, API keys, private logs, account data, or other sensitive information in public GitHub Issues or Discussions.

## Device Permissions

AG Mob may request the following permissions. You can manage permissions in iOS/iPadOS Settings.

### Camera

Used to scan Gateway setup codes and to take photos you choose to attach to a chat.

### Photos

Used to select photos or videos that you choose to attach.

### Microphone

Used only when you choose to record voice input or voice notes.

### Speech Recognition

Used only when you choose to turn speech into text. Depending on your device settings and platform behavior, speech recognition may be processed by Apple services or by the configured workflow.

### Files

Used to select documents or files that you choose to attach.

### Location

Used only when you explicitly choose to attach your current location. AG Mob does not track your location in the background.

### Notifications

Used for alerts such as run completion, approval requests, failures, and recovery events. Notification delivery may involve Apple Push Notification service and the Gateway or notification configuration used by your setup.

## How Information Is Used

Information handled by AG Mob is used to:

- Connect and reconnect to your selected Gateway
- Display chats, tasks, live runs, approvals, and results
- Send messages and attachments you choose to send
- Queue and recover messages when the connection is unavailable
- Store local preferences and cached content
- Provide support, investigate bugs, and improve product reliability
- Respond to security, privacy, or abuse reports

## Data Sharing

AG Mob may share or transmit information in these limited cases:

- To the OpenClaw Gateway you configure, so the app can provide its core features.
- To Apple services when required by iOS/iPadOS features such as notifications, App Store distribution, TestFlight, or speech recognition.
- To GitHub if you post public Issues or Discussions.
- To email providers if you contact support by email.
- To service providers or systems that your selected Gateway is configured to use.
- When required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or to protect users, the app, or the community.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

## User-Controlled Gateway Processing

AG Mob is built around a user-selected Gateway. The privacy and security of content processed by that Gateway depends on the Gateway owner, its configuration, and any services connected to it.

If you connect to a Gateway operated by someone else, that Gateway operator may be able to process, store, or review the content you send through it. Only connect to Gateways you own or trust.

If a review or test Gateway is provided for App Store review or beta testing, content sent to that Gateway may be processed for testing, support, and review operations.

## Data Retention

- Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear cache, disconnect the Gateway, uninstall the app, or the app removes it as part of normal operation.
- Gateway-side data is controlled by the Gateway owner and its configuration.
- Support emails and community reports may be retained as long as needed to respond, maintain records, prevent abuse, or improve reliability.

## Security

AG Mob uses platform-provided secure storage where available for sensitive connection credentials such as pairing tokens. The app also relies on iOS/iPadOS security features and the transport security of your configured Gateway connection.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Please report security or privacy issues privately to:

`aliendaniel@hotmail.com`

## Your Choices

You can:

- Choose which Gateway to connect to
- Disconnect or switch Gateway
- Clear local cache from the app where supported
- Manage app permissions in iOS/iPadOS Settings
- Avoid attaching photos, files, audio, or location if you do not want to share them
- Delete public GitHub posts or contact GitHub according to GitHub's policies
- Contact us about privacy questions at `aliendaniel@hotmail.com`

## Children

AG Mob is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support or community channels, contact `aliendaniel@hotmail.com`.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app, support process, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted in this repository with a new “Last updated” date.

## Contact

For privacy, support, or security questions, contact:

`aliendaniel@hotmail.com`
